ResearchPilot: Ask Research Papers, Get Answers You Can Verify

A RAG assistant that answers questions about research papers with page-level citations, and says "not enough evidence" when the papers don't contain the answer.

  • Upload PDFs and ask about one paper or several. Every answer cites the exact pages it came from, and the code checks each citation instead of trusting the AI.
  • Hybrid search (BM25 keyword search plus vector search, merged with Reciprocal Rank Fusion), then a reranker keeps the best five passages.
  • Tested on a 32-question set: finding the right page went from 64% to 96%, correct answers from 88% to 100%, and 7 of 7 unanswerable questions were correctly refused. 40+ automated tests.
